Assembly accessories

Please find the following accessories

  1. Hand tightening nuts *2 sets

2. latitude fine-tuning screw *1 set

3. “Protruding” fixed cover   * 4. M3 screw  * 4

 

First assemble the latitude adjusting screw

M3 screws are used here

If youNo large load requirements, it is not necessary to install the “convex” fixed cover. The bearing slot is equipped with a strong magnet, which will automatically absorb the screw assembly and will not fall off.

This makes it easy to carry and fold quickly when traveling.

If youThere is a need for large load capacity, you need to install the fixed cover, otherwise the work of fine-tuning the dimensions to fine-tune polar alignment will become difficult

Just tighten it, don't use too much force, otherwise the print may be damaged.

Assemble latitude locking knob and azimuth adjustment knob

The two knobs 1 and 2 are used to tighten the dimension angle after polar alignment.

The two knobs 3 and 4 are used to fine-tune the orientation during polar alignment

The dimension adjustment knob has 2 installation positions. Selecting different installation positions can obtain different latitude angle ranges.

For China lovers:

To the north of Changchun and Karamay, please select "Installation Location 1"

To the south of Changchun and Karamay, please select "Installation Location 2"

Areas south of 25 degrees north latitude (Kunming, Guangzhou) need to replace the low latitude adapter (the bracket is longer)

Or you can choose the low latitude special version.
