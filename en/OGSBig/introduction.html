Product structure

Use the top and bottom diagrams to identify the adjustment mechanisms, ports, and mounting points.

Top structure

  1. Gearbox belt tensioning screws (A / B)
  2. Belt tension adjustment screw
  3. Bubble level
  4. ¼-20 UNC ball-head mounting screw
  5. Laser / polar-scope mount
  6. Hinge shaft
  7. Latitude adjustment knob
  8. Azimuth adjustment knob

Bottom and interfaces

  1. 2.5 mm camera shutter port
  2. Tracking status indicator
  3. USB-C power input (5 V, at least 1 A)
  4. Removable phone holder
  5. Belt tension indicators (A / B)
  6. 3/8-inch tripod thread
  7. Magnetic storage point for the latitude adjustment lever
