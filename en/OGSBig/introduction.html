Product Structure

  1. Reduction gearbox belt tightening screw

    2. b Belt tightening screw B
  2. Belt tension adjustment screw
  3. bubble level
  4. ¼-20 UNC screws (for mounting ball head, use included ¼-20 to 3/8 adapter)

  6. hinge fixed shaft
  7. latitude adjustment knob
  8. Azimuth adjustment knob

9. Camera shutter cable interface (2.5mm jack)

10. Tracker status indicator light (flashes to indicate tracking starts)

11. USB-C power supply interface (5V, at least 1A)

12. Detachable mobile phone holder (for polar alignment using mobile phones)

13a. Belt Tension Indicator

13b. Belt Tension Indicator

14. 3/8-inch tripod thread (a 3/8 to ¼ adapter can also be used to connect smaller tripods)

15. latitude Adjustment lever magnetic position (can be used for storage when traveling)
