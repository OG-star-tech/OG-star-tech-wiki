# # What's included in the package?

Generally speaking, the package contains at least:

1. Equatorial mount body * 1
2. Star pointing pen * 1 (for coarse polar alignment, install the star pointing pen on the equatorial mount, and adjust the equatorial mount to point near the North Star)
3. Paper or electronic manual * 1

> **Additional notes:**
> 1. Depending on different activities or version upgrades, the type and quantity of accessories in the package may change.
> 2. The accessories not mentioned are the accessories officially given to you by OG Star for free. Thank you for your support of OG Star Community.
> 3. Search the OG Star Community for **modifications (MODs)**, or design your own modules to improve your imaging workflow.
{.is-info}


# # What extra preparations are needed?

In order for the equatorial mount to work properly, you need to prepare at least the following peripherals:

1. Ball head (for mounting the camera)
2. A stable and sturdy tripod
3. power bank and a stable USB-C data cable (for power supply)

> **For precise polar alignment, you can purchase a polar-scope module for a compatible equatorial mount and install it on the OGS tracker with the appropriate adapter.**
> *For example EQ5, EQ3, PT5C, etc.*

# # Disclaimer

1. Tripods, ball heads, cameras, mobile power supplies and data cables are not part of the product.
2. High-quality accessories can help improve the quality of your shooting, while inferior accessories may affect the performance of the device.
