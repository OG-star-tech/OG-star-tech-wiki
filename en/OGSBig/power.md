# OG Star Tracker Power Supply Guide

# # Power requirements

- Interface: USB-C
- Input voltage: 5V
- Power output capability: at least 1A
- Recommended power supply: reliable quality 5V power bank or regulated power supply

> Do not use non-compatible power supply solutions that output 9V, 12V or higher voltage to the device. Just because USB-C has the same appearance does not mean that the output voltage is necessarily suitable for this device.

# # Check before powering on

1. Make sure the camera, ball head and equatorial mount are securely installed.
2. Confirm that latitude and the azimuth adjustment mechanism are locked.
3. Verify that the power supply has a nominal output of 5V and is continuously delivering at least 1A.
4. Plug in the USB-C cable and wait for the device to boot up and broadcast Wi-Fi.

# # When unable to start

1. Replace the USB-C cable with a confirmed one that can transmit power.
2. Replace the power supply with a 5V/1A or higher current capability; "higher current capability" does not mean increasing the voltage.
3. Disconnect the camera shutter cable and other accessories, leaving only the equatorial mount powered and then test.
4. If there is still no response, do not disassemble the phone for live testing. Please contact after-sales service and record the power supply model, cable and indicator light status.
