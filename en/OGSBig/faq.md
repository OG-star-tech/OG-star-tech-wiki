# OG Star TrackerFAQ

# # Are the parameters of the domestic version and the overseas version different?

Currently, it is a unified version at home and abroad, and the core product parameters adopt the same caliber.

# # What is the maximum load?

The uniform caliber is 5 kg. Maximum load is not recommended for all scene workloads; the longer the focal length, the higher the wind, and the higher the center of gravity, the higher the requirements for tripod, balance, and polar accuracy.

# # Can it be powered by 9V or 12V?

No. The current unified power supply caliber is USB-C 5V, and the power supply capability is at least 1A.

# # The mobile phone shows that Wi-Fi has no Internet. Is it faulty?

No. There is a local connection between the mobile phone and the equatorial mount. After staying connected, visit `http://tracker.local` in the browser; if necessary, temporarily turn off mobile data or "automatically switch to an Internet-enabled network".

# # Can I use iPhone?

The web control interface can be used via a browser. The standalone application described in the instructions is currently for Android; functions and download channels may change with firmware updates.

# # Which tracking speed should be chosen?

- Sidereal: stars and most deep space targets.
- Lunar: Moon.
- Solar: sun. Use of this speed does not provide protection from solar viewing, qualified solar filters must still be used.

# # How to choose between laser, polar scope and smartphone polar alignment?

- polar scope: Usually the most accurate, suitable for longer focal lengths or longer exposures.
- Laser: Fast to operate, but generally less accurate than polar scope and subject to aviation and local regulations.
- Mobile phone assistance: suitable for beginners, in situations where the Polaris cannot be seen, near the equator or in the southern hemisphere; the manual recommends priority for focal lengths below 100 mm.
- Drift method: It takes longer, but can achieve higher accuracy.

# # Why can’t the exposure time in the instruction manual be directly used as a guarantee?

Exposure limit can be affected by polar error, focal length, pixel size, load balance, tripod, wind and ground vibration. The table can only be used as a test reference, and your own star point inspection results should prevail.
