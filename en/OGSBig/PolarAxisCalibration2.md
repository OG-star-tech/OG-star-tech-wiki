# 2. polar scopepolar alignment description (recommended for high accuracy)
![ishot_2026-04-11_22.39.17.png](/ogs赤道仪v/ishot_2026-04-11_22.39.17.png)

The polar scope alignment method is the **standard and the most accurate polar alignment method**, suitable for long-exposure photography.

This method supports:

**Multi-minute long exposure**

---

# Applicable scenarios

Recommended:

* star-field astrophotography
* Deep space photography
* Focal length ≥ 100mm
* Requires high precision tracking

Accuracy:

Better than laser and cell phone methods

---

# Required tools

One of the following applications needs to be installed:

* **Polar Clock**
* **Polar Scope**

These applications are used for:

Calculate the correct position of Polaris in polar scope.

---

# Operation steps

# # 1. Adjust the device to point north

Point the tripod roughly at:

**Due north direction (Northern Hemisphere)**

or:

**Due south (southern hemisphere)**

---

# # 2. Unlock the adjustment mechanism

Unscrew:

* latitude adjustment knob (up and down adjustment)
* Azimuth adjustment knob (left and right adjustment)

Make a rough adjustment.

---

# # 3. Open polar scope

Observed via polar scope:

Found:

**Polaris (Northern Hemisphere)**

or:

**South Celestial Pole Reference Sector**

---

# # 4. Use the app to determine location

Open:

Polar Clock or Polar Scope

The app will display:

**Polaris target position in polar scope**

---

# # 5. Adjust polar axis position

by:

* latitude adjustment
* Azimuth adjustment

Will:

**Polaris moves to the designated location**

Complete polar alignment.

---

# Usage suggestions

Recommended:

* All medium and telephoto shots
* Need stable long exposure
* Deep space photography

This is:

**The most recommended standard method**
