# Tripod and ball head installation instructions


# # 1. Required parts


* Ball head (for mounting the camera)
![Ball head.png](/配件（parts）/球头.png)
* Tripod (high strength model recommended)

---

# # 2. Installation steps

## # 1. Install the tracker to the tripod

First, fix the **3/8 threaded interface** at the bottom of the tracker to the tripod.

![ishot_2026-04-11_22.10.57.png](/ogs赤道仪v/ishot_2026-04-11_22.10.57.png)

* If the tripod is **3/8 threaded**:
The tracker can be installed directly.

* If the tripod is **1/4 thread**:
It is necessary to use **1/4 to 3/8 thread adapter** before installation.

When installing, make sure:

* Thread fully locked
* The connection is firm and without shaking
* The tripod is in a stable supporting state

**Suggested:**
Try to use the **tripod without a head or the tripod head** to avoid shaking caused by additional structures.

---

## # 2. Install the ball head

Attach the ball head to the top of the tracker.

![ishot_2026-04-11_22.11.05.png](/ogs赤道仪v/ishot_2026-04-11_22.11.05.png)

Since the top of the tracker uses a **1/4-20 thread**, use the included:

**1/4-20 to 3/8 inch adapter screw**

When installing:

* Tighten clockwise
* Appropriate pressure can be applied
* Make sure the gimbal is not loose

The camera can then be mounted on top of the ball head.

---

# 3. Why use a ball head (instead of a fluid head)

In star-field astrophotography, **ball head** is recommended, while **fluid head (video head)** is not recommended.

The main reasons are as follows:

## # 1. ball head has higher rigidity

The ball head has a simple structure:

* Almost no internal displacement after locking
* Strong resistance to micro deformation
* More suitable for long exposures

And the fluid head:

* Internal damping structure
* Long-term stress may cause slight slippage
* Will cause star points to smear

For star-field astrophotography, **micron-level displacement will affect the imaging quality**.

---

## # 2. The ball head is more suitable for quick composition.

star-field astrophotography requires:

* Quickly adjust direction
* Accurate lock posture

Ball head:

* Single knob lock
* High adjustment efficiency
* More suitable for field operations

Hydraulic PTZ is more suitable for:

* Video shooting
* Smooth motion following

Not suitable for starry sky photography scenes.

---

# 4. How to choose a suitable tripod

The stability of the tripod directly determines the tracking accuracy.

**A tripod with insufficient load-bearing capacity will seriously affect the shooting quality.**

---

# # Suggested selection criteria

Preference:

* Metal structure (tripod legs are recommended to be aluminum or carbon fiber)
* Rated load capacity ≥ **2–3 times the actual equipment weight**
* Equipped with **3/8 threaded interface**

For example:

If the total weight of the equipment is:

* Camera + lens + equatorial mount ≈ **3 kg**

Recommended tripod:

* Nominal load capacity ≥ **8–10 kg**

---

# # The impact of insufficient load-bearing

When the tripod is not rigid enough:

May appear:

* slight vibration
* Swaying in the wind
* Long term deformation

The final performance is:

* Star trailing
* The stars are not round
* Difficulty focusing
* Tracking accuracy decreases

Even if the equatorial mount itself performs well, **'s unstable tripod can still lead to failed shots.**

---

# 5. Why should we lower the tripod center column?

**It is recommended to lower the tripod center column as much as possible or completely retract it.**

This is one of the key factors affecting stability.

---

# # Raising the center column will reduce stability

When the center column is raised:

Equivalent to:

**Add a slender post to the top of the tripod**

This results in:

* Rigidity is significantly reduced
* Reduced torsion resistance
* Easier to shake

Especially in:

* Telephoto lens
* Windy environment
* long exposure

The impact is more obvious.

---

# # Recommended practices

Priority:

* Adjust tripod leg length
* Keep the center column in the lowest position
* Fully retract the center column if necessary

Only if:

**When the height is indeed insufficient**

I just considered slightly raising the center column.

---

# 6. Additional stability suggestions (recommended)

In order to obtain a higher shooting success rate, it is recommended to:

* Tripod fully extended
* Increase the tripod leg angle appropriately
* Hang a counterweight (such as a camera bag) under the tripod
* Avoid soft ground (sand, loose soil)

These measures can significantly improve tracking stability.

---

# 7. Summary of key principles

In star tracker system:

**stability takes priority above all else.**

Please always follow:

1. Use a highly rigid tripod
2. Use a ball head
3. Lower the center column
4. All threaded connections must be fully tightened

By doing the above four points, the success rate of star-field astrophotography can be significantly improved.
