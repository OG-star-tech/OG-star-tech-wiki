# Wi-Fi cannot be connected or the control page cannot be opened

# # Normal connection method

1. Connect the USB-C 5V power supply to the equatorial mount, with a power supply capacity of at least 1A.
2. Wait for the device to start and select `OG Star Tracker` in the phone Wi-Fi list.
3. Ignore the "No Internet connection on this network" prompt and stay connected.
4. Enter `http://tracker.local` in the browser address bar. Do not search for the address in the search box.

# # Check item by item

1. Turn off mobile data, VPN, and "Automatically switch to better network/cellular network".
2. Forget the Wi-Fi and reconnect.
3. Turn off the forced HTTPS function of the browser and confirm that you are using `http://`.
4. Replace the USB-C cable and reliable 5V power supply and power on again.
5. Use another mobile phone or computer to connect to distinguish the problem on the device side and the mobile side.
6. If you can connect to Wi-Fi but the page still cannot be opened, record the phone model, system version, browser, indicator light status and fault occurrence time, and then contact support.

# # What not to do

- Do not increase the supply voltage to "boost the signal".
- Do not flash firmware at will when there is no corresponding version of firmware and recovery method.
- Don’t mistake the “No Internet” prompt on your device’s Wi-Fi for an inevitable hardware failure.
